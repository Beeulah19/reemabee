Mobile product design · AI learning · Onboarding

Lytmus Onboarding UI

Lytmus is an AI-mentored learning app for NEET aspirants, designed to support students through video learning, doubt support, practice, progress, and study planning.

This onboarding was designed as a value-led experience, not a feature-led one. By understanding each student’s preferences, goals, and emotional needs without making them feel judged, the flow helps build trust early and gives them something meaningful in return - a more personal and supportive study journey.

The full case study is still cooking.

I’m currently putting together the complete story behind the research, user flows, interaction decisions, and visual direction that shaped this experience.
